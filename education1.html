My Educational Journey

Every Great Journey Begins with a Small Step

Education is not simply about attending classes, passing examinations, or earning certificates. For me, education is a continuous journey of discovering knowledge, developing new abilities, understanding the world, and becoming a better version of myself.

My story is still being written, but every chapter has contributed something valuable to the person I am becoming.

University Education

A New Chapter at Bahauddin Zakariya University

One of the most important chapters of my educational journey began when I entered university.

I am currently pursuing my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics at Bahauddin Zakariya University (BZU), Multan, Pakistan (Session 2025 – 2029) at the Center for Advanced Studies in Pure and Applied Mathematics (CASPAM).

University life introduced me to a different level of academic learning.

Unlike earlier educational stages, university education requires deeper understanding, independent study, analytical thinking, and the ability to connect different concepts.

My journey at BZU is helping me explore mathematics beyond basic formulas and calculations.

I am learning to understand mathematical structures, analyze complex problems, and approach questions through logical reasoning.

For me, university is not just about earning a degree. It is an opportunity to develop an intellectual foundation that can support my future in mathematics, computing, artificial intelligence, and technology.

My University Learning Experience

1. Calculus — Understanding Change and Motion

Calculus is one of the important areas of my mathematical education. Through calculus, I explore how quantities change, how functions behave, and how mathematical relationships can be used to understand real-world phenomena.

My studies include:

  • Limits and Continuity
  • Differentiation
  • Applications of Derivatives
  • Integration
  • Applications of Integration
  • Partial Derivatives
  • Multiple Integrals
  • Vector Calculus
  • Green's Theorem

I particularly enjoy understanding the logic behind differentiation and integration and exploring how calculus connects with physics, engineering, and computational sciences.

2. Linear Algebra — Understanding Mathematical Structures

Linear Algebra has introduced me to the language of vectors, matrices, and mathematical spaces. It helps me understand how mathematical systems can be represented and analyzed.

My areas of study include:

  • Matrices and Determinants
  • Systems of Linear Equations
  • Vector Spaces
  • Subspaces
  • Linear Independence
  • Basis and Dimension
  • Linear Transformations
  • Euclidean Spaces
  • Standard Unit Vectors

I find Linear Algebra particularly interesting because it has important applications in computer graphics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data science.

3. Ordinary Differential Equations — Understanding Dynamic Systems

Ordinary Differential Equations have helped me explore mathematical models involving changing quantities. This subject demonstrates how mathematical equations can describe physical, scientific, and engineering systems.

Through this area, I explore:

  • First-Order Differential Equations
  • Higher-Order Differential Equations
  • Linear Differential Equations
  • Initial Value Problems
  • Boundary Value Problems
  • Different Methods of Solving Differential Equations

I enjoy understanding the step-by-step logic behind solving differential equations and discovering their practical applications.

4. Modern Physics — Exploring the Scientific World

My academic interests also extend into modern physics. This subject has introduced me to fascinating concepts that explain the behavior of light, energy, and matter.

Some of the topics I have explored include:

  • Blackbody Radiation
  • Planck's Radiation Law
  • Photoelectric Effect
  • Compton Effect
  • Wave-Particle Duality
  • Electromagnetic Radiation
  • X-rays and Diffraction
  • Pair Production
  • Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle
  • Quantum Tunneling

These topics have expanded my understanding of the physical world and strengthened my interest in the relationship between mathematics and scientific discoveries.

5. Programming and Computational Learning

One of the most exciting aspects of my educational development is exploring programming alongside mathematics. I am interested in understanding how mathematical concepts can be implemented through computer programs.

My programming interests include:

  • Python Programming
  • C++
  • JavaScript
  • HTML and CSS
  • SQL and Database Fundamentals

I enjoy exploring mathematical problems through Python, understanding algorithms, and developing small applications. I believe computational knowledge can make mathematical learning more practical and open new opportunities in research, artificial intelligence, and software development.
